# Aguiar 1994 historical Katukina-Pano glossary transcription

This is a searchable transcription of every Katukina→Portuguese entry detected in the dissertation’s 41-page mini-glossary (PDF pages 329–369). It is kept separate from the modern Noke Koĩ list because the 1994 work uses older spellings and the author describes the glossary as preliminary and not fully revised.

**How to read it:** the **1994 form** and **Portuguese gloss** transcribe the scanned source; the **English guide** is a machine-assisted learner translation. OCR can confuse similar letters, so verify any form used for teaching, publication, or conversation against the linked scan and, ideally, with a fluent speaker. Class labels are normalized where the OCR was clear; a question mark means the abbreviation was unclear.

Class key: **N** noun, **V** verb, **ADJ** adjective, **ADV** adverb, **PRO** pronoun, **QTF** quantifier, and **T/A** tense/aspect marker. Entries remain in the source's alphabetical order.
